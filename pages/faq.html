FAQ

What exactly is a match cut?

A cut where one element stays in the same screen position while everything around it changes. Here the focal word you mark with == == stays centered; paper, typeface, rotation and filler text jump each frame.

Why is the focal word limited to 23 characters?

So it fits at 6% of frame width without overflowing at large rotations. Longer strings get a validation error and a red counter.

What formats can I export?

MP4 (H.264 + AAC via WebCodecs + mp4-muxer) when your browser supports VideoEncoder; otherwise WebM (VP9) via MediaRecorder. You choose a preference, we fall back automatically. Both are playable in Chrome, VLC, QuickTime (MP4) or Chrome/Firefox (WebM).

Which browsers work?

Latest Chrome/Edge handle MP4 fastest. Firefox and older Chrome fall back to WebM. Safari can preview but may not encode — you’ll get a clear error. All browsers can preview the animation; encoding is the only feature-gated part. No install needed.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Fonts are self-hosted OFL, paper is procedural noise, sound is synthesized. Open DevTools Network: zero requests during Generate or Download. Everything is Blob URLs in memory.

Can I use this commercially?

Yes. The app is MIT, no watermark, no attribution required (though appreciated). Your phrase is yours. See Terms.

Why 12 cuts per second by default?

It’s the sweet spot: fast enough to feel like a cut, slow enough to read the focal word. You can push to 4 (contemplative) or 30 (machine-gun). Zoom and blur ramp in the last 20% of the timeline for a subtle push-in.

My video is blank / download fails?

Check that you have exactly one ==word==. Try Regenerate to re-seed, or toggle format to WebM. If you’re on iOS Safari, WebCodecs is not yet available — WebM may also be limited.
