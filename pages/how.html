How it works

100% browser-only. No uploads, no servers, no watermark.

1 — You write a phrase

Wrap one word with ==double equals==. Keep it to 23 characters max — that’s your focal word. It’s the anchor. Everything else is allowed to wobble.

2 — We build a timeline

MatchCutter slices 2 seconds into frames at your chosen cuts-per-second (4–30, default 12). Each frame gets a different paper grain, font (six OFL faces), rotation (±2°), filler sentence, and highlight. A pure function picks the next layout — never the same preset twice in a row — using a seeded mulberry32 PRNG so Regenerate is repeatable.

3 — The anchor stays put

Every frame measures your focal word with Canvas measureText and offsets it so its centre lands exactly at (cx, cy). The paper rotates and zooms around that point, but the word itself is pixel-aligned. That’s the match cut.

4 — Sound is synthesized

Six effects — paper shuffle, camera shutter, film advance, polaroid, flash pop, none — are built from noise, filters and envelopes in Web Audio. No samples are fetched. Mixdown schedules one burst per cut in an OfflineAudioContext at 48 kHz.

5 — Export

Frames are painted to a hidden canvas, captured via captureStream + MediaRecorder (WebM) or VideoEncoder + mp4-muxer (MP4). Audio is mixed in. The encoder prefers MP4 when WebCodecs is available, otherwise falls back to WebM. Full resolution is 1080p per your aspect (9:16, 1:1, 16:9); preview is half-res for speed.

Why browser-only?

No queue, no cost, no privacy trade. Close the tab, your phrase is gone. Offline after the first load thanks to static assets. If VideoEncoder is missing, you still get WebM.
