Privacy

Updated 2026-10-04

MatchCutter is 100% client-side. Your phrase, timeline, audio mix and exported video never leave your browser. There is no backend, no user account, no storage.

Data we do not collect

No analytics, no ads, no trackers, no cookies, no localStorage for personal data, no third-party fonts or CDNs. Fonts are self-hosted, paper textures are generated, sounds are synthesized. SupportGate, if enabled, is a local placeholder with no network call.

What stays on your device

  • Your input text exists only in the input element and in-memory timeline.
  • Encoded video is a Blob URL created with URL.createObjectURL and revoked after download.
  • If you change controls, values live only in the page; reload resets to defaults.

Network requests

On first load the browser fetches index.html, styles/main.css, src/main.ts (bundled as JS) and OFL fonts. During Generate, Regenerate, Mixdown and Export zero additional requests are made. You can verify in DevTools → Network: filter to Fetch/XHR, then hit Generate → Download — nothing new should appear.

Third parties

None. No embedded videos, maps, or analytics.
